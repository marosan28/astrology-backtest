# COMPARATIVE MUNDANE ASTROLOGY RESEARCH PROTOCOL
## Version 1.0 — Preregistration Document

### PART 1: RESEARCH DESIGN AND SCOPE

**1.1 Central Research Question**

Which of four specified models — Tropical Western mundane astrology (Model A), Sidereal Western mundane astrology (Model B), Traditional Jyotish mundane astrology (Model C), or a non-astrological historical baseline (Model D) — demonstrates the strongest specific, repeatable, and falsifiable correspondence with documented collective historical and cultural changes?

**1.2 Pilot Scope**

Transit families under test:
1. Saturn–Neptune conjunctions, squares, and oppositions
2. Uranus–Pluto conjunctions, squares, and oppositions
3. Pluto sign ingresses
4. Uranus sign ingresses
5. Neptune sign ingresses

Historical period: 1800 CE through 2025 CE inclusive.

Limitation notice: The number of complete cycles within this window is small. Saturn–Neptune hard aspects recur approximately every 9–10 years, yielding roughly 20–25 instances. Uranus–Pluto hard aspects recur every 35–45 years, yielding roughly 5–6 instances. Pluto sign ingresses recur every 12–30 years, yielding roughly 10–11 instances. Uranus sign ingresses recur every 7 years, yielding roughly 32 instances. Neptune sign ingresses recur every 14 years, yielding roughly 16 instances. The small number of Uranus–Pluto instances means statistical significance cannot be achieved; any positive result must be treated as preliminary and pattern-suggestive only.

### PART 2: MODEL SPECIFICATIONS

**Model A — Tropical Western Mundane Astrology**
- Zodiac: Tropical (vernal equinox = 0° Aries)
- Planets: Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto
- Eclipses: Within 3° of a planet or node under study, within the aspect window; upgrades intensity by 1 point maximum
- Aspects: Conjunction (0°), square (90°), opposition (180°)
- Tradition: Baigent/Campion/Harvey 1984; Campion 1995; Tarnas 2006

**Model B — Sidereal Western Mundane Astrology**
- Zodiac: Sidereal, Fagan–Bradley ayanamsa (~24°51' as of J2000.0)
- Planetary meanings, aspects, orbs, scoring: Identical to Model A
- Purpose: Isolate whether tropical or sidereal sign symbolism produces stronger correspondence

**Model C — Traditional Jyotish Mundane Astrology**
- Ayanamsa: Lahiri (~23°51')
- Outer planets excluded from core; included only in separate C-Modern Supplement column
- Rāśis as primary territorial/thematic frame
- Nakshatras as secondary timing/qualitative layer
- Rahu/Ketu: shadow planets with defined mundane significations
- Planetary aspects: Jyotish dṛṣṭi (Saturn 3rd/7th/10th; Jupiter 5th/7th/9th; Mars 4th/7th/8th)
- Sources: Bṛhat Saṃhitā, BPHS, Sārāvalī, Phala Dīpikā

**Model D — Non-Astrological Historical Baseline**
Six causal categories: economic conditions, demographics, technology, political institutions, geopolitical pressures, environmental pressures.

### PART 3: TIMING RULES (LOCKED)

**Aspect Orbs:**
- Conjunction: 8° applying and separating
- Square: 6° applying and separating
- Opposition: 6° applying and separating
- Jyotish conjunctions: 5°; rāśi-based full aspects for traditional dṛṣṭi

**Applying vs. Separating:** Not scored differently for outcome category prediction. Applying = build-up; Peak (within 1°) = acute expression; Separating = institutionalisation/aftermath.

**Event Counting Windows:** Event may count if it begins within the orb window, or if clearly initiated within the orb window. Events beginning after separating orb closes do not count. Pre-window trends count only if they undergo a documented qualitatively distinct phase change within the window, recorded in scholarly literature independently.

**Ingress Windows:** 3 months before exact ingress through 12 months after. Retrograde re-entries: each treated as a distinct ingress with its own window. Overlapping windows treated as single continuous period, scored once.

**Hard Prohibition:** No event window may be widened retrospectively.

### PART 5: LOCKED ASTROLOGICAL HYPOTHESES

**Saturn–Neptune aspects:** Primary predicted categories: IRR, MSU, RMP (Neptune dissolving Saturn), IWT, CAS. Direction determined by pre-condition rule: if Saturn dominant → idealism captured/institutionalised; if Neptune dominant or in Neptune-ruled sign → dissolution of structures by collectivist/spiritual movements; otherwise → ambiguous.

**Uranus–Pluto aspects:** Primary predicted categories: RMP, TCT, IWT, AC. Intensity 4–5. Directional pre-condition: Jupiter within 15° of either → constructive transformation; Saturn/Mars within 15° → destructive/coercive; neither → ambiguous valence.

**Pluto ingresses:** Domain-specific transformation concentrated in sign-relevant area; most fundamental restructuring of that domain during transit. Sign-to-domain mapping locked as stated in protocol.

**Uranus ingresses:** Rapid disruption/innovation in sign-relevant domain. Intensity 2–3.

**Neptune ingresses:** Rise of idealistic/spiritual/utopian movements in sign-relevant domain; also collective delusion or propaganda risk in that domain. Intensity 2–3.

### PART 6: SCORING SYSTEM (LOCKED)

Nine dimensions, each 0–5:
1. Specificity
2. Timing accuracy
3. Replication across cycles
4. Discriminatory power
5. Contradiction resistance
6. Quality of historical evidence
7. Independence of evidence
8. Control period performance
9. Non-astrological baseline performance

Complexity penalty: deductions for extra variables, wider orbs, longer windows, contradictory meanings, post-hoc adjustments.

A more complex model must score ≥1.5 points higher than a simpler model to be ranked above it after penalty.

---

### PART 7: CONTROL METHODOLOGY (LOCKED)

1. Randomly shifted windows (5 per transit, shifts 18–60 months)
2. Outcomes without transits (at least 2 per category)
3. Transits without outcomes (full count, ratio recorded)
4. Shuffled transit dates
5. Non-astrological baseline comparison

---

### PART 8: EVIDENCE HANDLING RULES (LOCKED)

Clustering rule: dependent trends scored as single evidence unit.
Evidentiary hierarchy: L1–L6 (L6 cannot be used as evidence).
Scope: GL, MN, NA-large count; RG, DM, NC, AN do not count toward positive scores.
Full event log required per window; cherry-picking prohibited.
Geographic ethnocentrism rule: US/Europe-only events not automatically treated as global.
